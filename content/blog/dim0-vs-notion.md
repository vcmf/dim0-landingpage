## Quick take (read this first)

**The idea in two sentences.** Notion organizes your work into pages, databases,
and a left-hand tree. Dim0 spreads it across an infinite canvas where notes,
sketches, code, and a board-aware AI agent all live next to each other, and the
agent reads the whole board before it acts.

**Where Dim0 wins**
- Spatial, infinite canvas instead of a linear page tree (you can *see* how ideas relate). Notion has no native canvas.
- A board-aware AI agent native to a spatial surface: it reads the surrounding notes, sketches, and data, searches, runs code, and writes results back as editable nodes anywhere on the board.
- Mini-apps: real interactive React tools generated on the board (a calculator, a chart, a quiz).
- Open-source (MIT) and self-hostable. Your notes are plain Markdown; nothing is trapped.
- Freehand sketching and diagrams the agent can read as context.

**Where Notion wins**
- Databases are its superpower. Relations, rollups, filtered views, multiple layouts. Dim0 has nothing like it.
- AI that has caught up fast. Since Notion 3.0 (2025), Notion Agents run autonomous, multi-step tasks across your docs and databases. The AI gap is not the story anymore; the canvas is.
- A gigantic template and integration ecosystem, plus mature mobile and desktop apps.
- Best-in-class for structured docs, wikis, and team knowledge bases.
- Years of polish and stability at scale.

**Pick Dim0 if** you think spatially, you want AI that operates *on* your work
instead of beside it, and open-source / self-hosting matters to you.
**Pick Notion if** your work is fundamentally databases, wikis, and structured
documents, and you want the most mature, template-rich workspace available.

## The job you're actually trying to do

Most people don't want "a notes app." They want to take a messy idea, pull in
some sources, think it through, maybe sketch it, ask an AI for help, and end up
with something structured. Notion and Dim0 both promise to be the place that
happens. They just disagree about the *shape* of thinking.

## What Notion is (fairly)

Notion is an all-in-one workspace built around the **page** and the **database**.
Everything is a block; pages nest into pages; databases turn those pages into
tables, boards, calendars, and galleries with relations and rollups. Notion AI
can draft, summarize, and answer questions across your workspace, and since
Notion 3.0 (2025) it can run autonomous multi-step **Agents** that operate on
your docs and databases. It is, genuinely, the most complete structured-document
tool most teams will ever use.

Its model is **hierarchical and linear**. You navigate a tree on the left, and a
document top to bottom. That's a strength for reference material and a constraint
for early, tangled thinking.

## What Dim0 is

Dim0 is a **thinking canvas**. Instead of a page tree, you get an infinite board
where every kind of node coexists: Notion-grade rich notes, freehand shapes,
code nodes, charts, uploaded docs, nested boards, and AI-generated mini-apps. The
differentiator is the **agent**: it reads what's already on the board, goes and
does the work (search, run code, synthesize), and writes the result back as nodes
you can edit and connect. It's real-time collaborative, runs 8 AI models, and is
open-source and self-hostable.

Its model is **spatial**. You keep structure, relationships, and half-formed
ideas visible at once, which is how complex thinking actually works.

## Where Notion is genuinely better

Be honest: if your work lives in **databases**, Notion is not close to being
matched. Product roadmaps, CRMs, content calendars, anything relational. Notion's
template gallery, integrations, and mobile apps are years ahead. For a company
wiki or a structured knowledge base that many people read, Notion is the safer
call today.

## Where Dim0 pulls ahead

The moment your work is **exploratory and visual**, the page tree fights you.
Mapping a decision, researching a topic, sketching a system, comparing options
side by side. On a canvas you lay it out in space and the AI works *inside* that
space. Three things Notion structurally can't do:

1. **The AI works in space.** Both tools have capable agents now; the difference
   is the surface they act on. Dim0's agent operates across notes, sketches, code,
   and charts laid out on a canvas, not a linear doc or a database row.
2. **Mini-apps.** Describe a tool and get a real, interactive React app as a node,
   not a static block.
3. **You own everything.** MIT-licensed, self-hostable, notes in plain Markdown.

## So which should you use?

- **Reference, databases, team wiki:** Notion.
- **Thinking, research, planning, anything spatial, AI that acts:** Dim0.
- **Both is fine.** Plenty of people keep Notion as the system of record and use
  Dim0 as the place they actually think, then export the Markdown into Notion.
